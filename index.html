    Frequently asked questions

    What is Spot and Travel?

    A free website for discovering real places to visit. You swipe licensed photos and choose Travel or Skip. It doesn’t sell tours or bookings itself.

    Is Spot and Travel the same as Spot Travel?

    No. Spot and Travel (spotandtravel.com) has no connection to tour agencies or travel companies named “Spot Travel.”

    Are the photos real?

    Yes. Every card uses a licensed real photograph (mostly Wikimedia Commons, Creative Commons licenses). No AI images are used, and the credits appear under each card.

    Do I need to sign up?

    No. Swiping is free and needs no account. A weekly “hidden gem” email is optional.

    How do I get my place listed?

    Use Submit a Place. Listings cost $39.99 for the founding first year (one-time), $49.99/year for a standard listing, or $99.99 for 12 months in Hidden Gems. We check every listing for accuracy before it goes live. Card payments are only held, and the card is charged only if the listing is approved. Full details are on our Support page.

    What is a pin?

    An optional add-on that keeps your card among the first 25 cards in its tab. A 7-day pin is +$39.99 and a 30-day pin is +$99.99. Pins depend on availability, with a cap of 25 per tab, and the pin clock starts when your card goes live, not when you pay. Full details are on our Support page.

    Can my business sponsor a category?

    Yes. Each category page has one sponsor spot at a time, clearly labeled “Sponsored,” for $25 for 30 days (launch price). See Sponsor a Category.

    How does the random destination generator work?

    Pick where you’re based, filter by domestic or international and by category, then spin to get a real place. Try the Random Travel Destination Generator.
