Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This explains what Spot and Travel (run by Alan Huesers, P.O. Box 143, Hartington, NE 68739) collects, why, and what you can do about it. Short version: we collect as little as we can, and we never sell your data.

1. Browsing and swiping

  • We count visits using a random ID kept in your browser's storage. Our server only stores hashed versions of it, along with the page you opened and the site that linked you (domain only).
  • We count Travel/Skip swipes per place to show what's trending.
  • Counts may be tagged with your approximate country, worked out by our hosting provider (Cloudflare) from your connection. We don't store your IP address or exact location.
  • The home country you set on the wheel, your saved lists, your language choice and your swipe tallies stay in your browser on your device. We don't receive them.

2. Cookies and similar storage

The site doesn't set advertising or tracking cookies and doesn't use Google Analytics or similar third-party analytics. It uses your browser's local storage for the items above. You can clear it any time in your browser settings. Sites you visit through our booking links may set their own cookies.

3. Forms you send us

When you submit a place, contact support, suggest a place or ask for a correction or removal, we receive what you type, including your name and email. Our forms are processed by Basin (usebasin.com), which delivers them to us by email. We use this only to review your request and reply.

4. Weekly email

If you sign up for the weekly email, we store your email address only to send it. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email, or by emailing hello@spotandtravel.com. We currently send it ourselves from hello@spotandtravel.com.

5. Payments

  • Card: handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. Stripe shares limited details with us (such as your name, email and the amount) and handles your data under its own privacy policy.
  • Crypto: handled through the Xaman app on the XRP Ledger. We never have access to your wallet or keys. To match your payment to your listing, we keep an order record (reference, amount, currency, place name and the email you gave). The place name and email are deleted on the schedule below; the payment details are kept as a tax record. Ledger transactions, including your wallet address, are public on the XRP Ledger by design and can't be deleted by anyone.

6. Booking and affiliate links

Some links go to partners like Expedia through affiliate programs (for example Travelpayouts). When you click one, the partner may use cookies or link codes to know you came from us, so we can earn a commission. What happens on their site is covered by their privacy policy.

7. How long we keep it

  • Listings and pins: when a listing or pin ends, we keep its details inactive for 30 days in case you renew. If you don't, we delete them.
  • Declined listings and unpaid orders: deleted 30 days after they're declined, refunded or expire.
  • Support and form messages: deleted 30 days after the request is resolved or declined.
  • Payment and tax records: we keep only what tax and legal rules require (amount, date, payment reference and receipt), for as long as they require it. Stripe keeps its own records under its policy, and crypto transactions stay on the public XRP Ledger.
  • Weekly email: your address is kept until you unsubscribe, then deleted.
  • Anonymous counts: visit and swipe counts can't identify you and may be kept longer.

8. Sharing

We don't sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with the services that run the site (Cloudflare, Basin, Stripe, Xaman, our email provider), when the law requires it, or to protect the site from fraud or abuse.

9. Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing hello@spotandtravel.com. We'll reply within 2 business days and act on it within a reasonable time. Payment records we must keep for tax purposes, and public ledger data, can't be deleted.

10. Children

The site isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you think a child has sent us information, email us and we'll delete it.

11. Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. Questions: hello@spotandtravel.com or Alan Huesers, P.O. Box 143, Hartington, NE 68739. See also our Terms and Conditions.
