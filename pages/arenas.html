    About Spot and Travel. Swipe real, visitable places — Travel if you’d go, Skip if you wouldn’t. Photos are licensed real photographs (no AI images).

    Privacy: Spot and Travel counts visits anonymously, using a random ID stored in your browser that we only keep in hashed form, the page you opened and the website that linked you (domain only). It also counts Travel/Skip swipes per place to show which destinations are trending. Counts may be tagged with your approximate country, but we never store your IP address or exact location, or the home country you set on the wheel. If you sign up for the weekly email, we store your email address only to send it; we never sell it, and you can unsubscribe anytime. Your saved lists stay on this device.
